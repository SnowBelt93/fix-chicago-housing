Support Shape Chicago

Shape Chicago is free, has no accounts and never will. If it helped you understand your city, you can chip in to keep it running.

What it pays for

  • Hosting and the domain. The maps, data files and plan gallery run on Cloudflare.
  • Keeping the data fresh. The permit tracker, ward records and news list rebuild themselves every day; that takes computing time.
  • New tools. More of the city's big questions, done the same way: every number linked to its source.

What it doesn't buy

Influence. Supporters don't get a say in what the tools show, and no number changes for anyone. If you'd rather put your business in front of people who use the site, see advertising.
