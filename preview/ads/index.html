Sponsor spots: five options

Each spot is built for this site rather than a generic ad box. They sit where people are reading, not where they're working in a tool, and each one says plainly that it's a sponsor. Pick the ones you like; the advertise page will then describe only those.

  • Never on the maps, inside a simulator while someone is using it, or on Your address pages (people make real decisions there).
  • One sponsor per spot, labeled, plain link, no tracking. The businesses shown below are made up.

1. Ward sponsor

Your ward page, under the alderperson

One local business per ward, shown only on that ward's page and in the ward's feed. A bakery in Ward 1 reaches the people who follow Ward 1, the most local audience the site has.

  • 50 spots, sold ward by ward
  • Must be in the ward
  • Never on wards' votes or rezoning lists

2. Section underwriter

Top of a Progress section, under the title

Like public radio: "Homes added is supported by…", one short line and a small logo. Fits firms that want to be seen backing good information, such as architects, builders, credit unions and foundations.

  • Eight sections, one underwriter each
  • Underwriters never see numbers early or change them

3. Card in the news

News page and the home page's "This week", once per page

Looks like the other news cards, with a dashed edge and a "Local sponsor" label, after the eighth story. Good for events, openings and nonprofits' calls to action.

  • One at a time, changes weekly
  • Never made to look like a headline

4. Classifieds on the front page

Bottom of the housing and budget newspaper front pages

The results pages already look like a newspaper, so the sponsors become its classifieds: three small boxed notices under the box score. People share these front pages, so the classifieds travel with them (the share image leaves them off).

  • Three slots, rotating daily
  • Text only, newspaper type
