Who can advertise

  • Chicago businesses and nonprofits. Based here, run here.
  • Not: political campaigns, PACs, ballot committees or lobbyists; anyone with a zoning change, contract or ordinance in front of the City Council; payday lenders, gambling or cannabis.
  • Sponsors never change what the tools show. Every card is labeled "Local sponsor".

How it works

A card is your name, one line and a link (plus a logo if you like). It's a plain link: we don't track who sees or clicks it, so you'll get a count of page views for the pages it's on, not clicks. Cards run by the month; if more than one sponsor shares a spot, they take turns by day.
