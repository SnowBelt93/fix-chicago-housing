Shape Chicago is a free, fan-made set of simulations for learning how housing and transit in Chicago work. By using it, you agree to these terms.

These are estimates, not advice

The tools use public data, published research and stated assumptions to estimate what might happen. Real results depend on markets, politics and many things a model can't capture. Nothing here is legal, financial, investment or planning advice, and nothing here is an official forecast. Every number links to its source or is labeled as an assumption, so check the source before relying on it.
