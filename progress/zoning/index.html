No official target

The city has no target for how fast a rezoning turns into homes

Its Cut the Tape initiative, begun in 2024, is the program meant to speed approvals and permits. It reports steps taken, not a number to hit. Cut the Tape ›

A zoning change is the Council changing what can be built on a lot. Why “at least”: the zoning map keeps only the latest change on each site, so earlier ones are lost and the true share is higher. The table counts the 341 zoning changes since 2021 (of 2,093) whose application clearly describes a new residential building and no conversion of an existing one. A permit counts if it is for new construction inside the rezoned area on the zoning map, or at the same address, on or after the vote. Among those that got a permit, half got it within 9 months. For 2025, the 12-month figure covers the 60 passed by early November 2025.
