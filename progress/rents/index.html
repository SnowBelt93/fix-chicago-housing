No official target

No city program sets a target for rents or sale prices

Rents and prices are the result the other sections add up to, so this section is a readout, not a scorecard.

Asking rent is Zillow’s Observed Rent Index for the city: rents advertised for homes on the market, all sizes, smoothed. It runs above what sitting tenants pay. The 2026-dollar figures use the national Consumer Price Index (yearly averages; 2026 is the average of the months published so far, so the last step is an estimate). Sale prices are the median of what sold that year, not the same homes. They come from the Cook County Assessor’s sales in Chicago, leaving out sales under $10,000, resales within a year, unusual deeds and multi-parcel sales.
