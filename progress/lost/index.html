How we count teardowns: each demolition permit is matched to the nearest building in the city’s 2013 footprints (within 25 m) to read how many homes it held. Other fair ways of matching give 660 to 860 for 2025. Most teardowns are followed by a new building, which the permit count includes. Renovation figures come from permits whose wording states the change (for example “convert from 3 units to 2”), leaving out permits that expired or were cancelled. We catch roughly half of the cuts and fewer of the additions, so both are floors and the two should not be subtracted.
