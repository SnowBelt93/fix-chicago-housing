The official goal

Vision Zero: no traffic deaths or serious injuries by 2026

The city’s 2017 Vision Zero Action Plan set 2026 as its deadline to eliminate traffic deaths and serious injuries. Through Sept. 27, 82 people had died this year, and 1,537 had been seriously hurt through Nov. 14. The action plan ›

Deaths are the city’s reviewed Vision Zero list, which is published about a month behind. Serious injuries come from police crash reports, which count a little differently (they show 89 deaths through Nov. 14, against 78 a year earlier). Pothole figures cover finished repairs only; open requests are not in the data, and counts follow the winter as much as the crews. The repair time covers requests finished in the last 12 months.
