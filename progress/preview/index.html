Chicago permitted 3,761 homes in the last 12 months. Closing its share of the shortage by 2041 takes 10,379 a year.

Updated Nov. 14, 2026 · a preview: the current Progress overview stays until you approve this one

At this pace

At the last 12 months’ pace, the gap doesn’t close: it grows from 54,141 homes today to about 99,000 by 2041.

Holding the gap where it is takes 6,770 homes a year (the 3,908 built in a normal year plus the 2,862 a year the shortage grows); closing it by 2041 takes 10,379. The pace and the goal are the housing simulator’s, an estimate.
