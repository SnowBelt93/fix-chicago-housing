The pace is the housing simulator’s goal, an estimate: Chicago’s share of the regional shortage, closed by 2041. Over 2010–2025 our total is within 1% of the Census permit survey; single years differ by 8% on average. Torn-down homes are an estimate through Nov. 6: each demolition permit is matched to the building it removed (other fair ways of matching give 660 to 860 for 2025), and most teardowns are followed by a new building that the permit count includes. Homes per ward come from the 2020 Census. “Finished” means the city marked the permit complete or issued a certificate of occupancy. Coach house applications are counted from Mar. 31, 2026; the 2021–2025 pilot cleared 907.
