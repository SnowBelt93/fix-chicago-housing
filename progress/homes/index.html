No official target

The city has not set a citywide target for new homes

The pace on this page is our own yardstick: Chicago’s share of the region’s housing shortage, closed by 2041 (10,379 homes a year). The nearest official plan is the 2024–2028 Housing and Economic Development Bond, which projects 600 to 1,000 affordable rental homes, 750 homes on empty lots in neighborhoods and 600 or more mixed-income homes every five years from a new city fund. The bond plan ›

The pace is the housing simulator’s goal, an estimate: Chicago’s share of the regional shortage, closed by 2041. Over 2010–2025 our permit total is within 1% of the Census permit survey; single years differ by 8% on average. Torn-down homes are an estimate, counted every morning through the newest demolition permit (Nov. 9): each permit is matched to the building at its address in the city’s 2013 footprints (the one its point falls in, or the nearest within 12 m) to read how many homes it held. Permits for only a garage or porch are left out, and so are matches to a building much bigger than the one the permit describes. Looser or tighter matching moves a year’s figure by about a tenth. Most teardowns are followed by a new building, which the permit count includes. Renovation figures come from permits whose wording states the change (for example “convert from 3 units to 2”), leaving out permits that expired or were cancelled. We catch roughly half of the cuts and fewer of the additions, so both are floors, and neither is subtracted or added in the net figure. Homes per ward come from the 2020 Census. “Finished” means the city marked the permit complete or issued a certificate of occupancy. Coach house applications are counted from Mar. 31, 2026; the 2021–2025 pilot cleared 907. The city comparison uses the Census Building Permits Survey’s yearly place file and the population it carries for each place; Jacksonville, Indianapolis and Charlotte are filed under their county governments there and are left out. New York is its five boroughs added together. Suburbs are Illinois places of 25,000 or more people in the Chicago metro area.
