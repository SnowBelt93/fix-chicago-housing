Chicago runs four pension funds, for city workers, laborers, police and firefighters. Together they hold about 27 cents for every dollar they owe, and state law makes the city pay more every year until they reach 90% in 2055 to 2058. Change how much the city pays, how fast, and how the money is invested to see what it costs and when the funds get there.
