For reporters

Every chart below can go in your story. Each one updates itself when the city or the federal government publishes new numbers, and each lists its sources.

Using our charts and numbers

Our charts, tables and the figures we calculate are free to use, quote and embed. Please credit Shape Chicago and link to the page the number came from. The underlying data belongs to the agencies that publish it; each chart names them.

Shape Chicago is a fan-made project. It is not affiliated with the City of Chicago, CTA, Metra or Pace, and it takes no side in elections. Estimates are labeled as estimates, and each section ends with how we count.

Spotted something wrong? Send a correction. We fix errors and say what changed.

How the simulators work

The housing, transit, budget and pensions simulators are estimates, not forecasts. Each lists its sources and every assumption it makes: open Sources in the header of any simulator. Plans that people share are on Plans people made, with a summary of the most common choices on What people picked.
