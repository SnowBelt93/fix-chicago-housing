## Riders who used to drive, trips, emissions

- LA Expo Line Ph 2 survey: 70% new riders, 44% of them used to drive (~31% of all riders). https://www.masstransitmag.com/rail/press-release/12257690/
- CTA Orange Line (TRR 1503, 1995): ~25% of boardings new to transit with a car available. https://trid.trb.org/View/453012
- LA Metro Rapid (BRT-lite): 17% previously made no trip or used a non-transit mode. https://onlinepubs.trb.org/onlinepubs/tcrp/tcrp90v1_cs/LosAngeles.pdf
- Assumption for the tool: 15–30% of riders diverted from driving (low for bus, high for new rail).
- NHTS 2022: 1.5 occupants per household vehicle trip; 11.48 mi average vehicle trip. https://www.energy.gov/cmei/vehicles/articles/fotw-1333-march-11-2024-2022-average-number-occupants-trip-household ; https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/73764/dot_73764_DS1.pdf
- APTA 2023 average transit trip: bus 3.7 mi, heavy rail 4.4, light rail 5.1, streetcar 1.9, commuter rail 22.6. https://www.apta.com/wp-content/uploads/APTA-2025-Public-Transportation-Fact-Book.pdf
- EPA: typical car ~400 g CO2 per mile. https://www.epa.gov/greenvehicles/greenhouse-gas-emissions-typical-passenger-vehicle
- FTA GHG factors (t CO2e per vehicle-mile): heavy rail car 0.00205, light rail/streetcar 0.00289, electric commuter rail 0.00387, diesel commuter rail 0.02717, diesel bus 0.00196, electric bus 0.00104. https://transit.dot.gov/sites/fta.dot.gov/files/2024-04/Greenhouse-Gas-Emissions-from-Transit-Projects-Programmatic-Assessment-Report-0263.pdf

## Riders vs. how often service runs

- TCRP Report 95, Ch. 9 (Transit Scheduling and Frequency, 2004): average response "approximates an elasticity of +0.5" in service quantity (p. 9-4); recent observations group near +0.3 or +1.0 (p. 9-5). Headway elasticity by prior headway (Table 9-2, p. 9-8): −0.22 under 10 min, −0.46 for 10–50 min, −0.58 over 50 min; less response where service was already frequent (p. 9-11). https://libraryarchives.metro.net/DPGTL/2026-heavrin/2004-TCRP-system-changes-handbook-ch-09.pdf (also https://www.trb.org/publications/tcrp/tcrp_rpt_95c9.pdf)
- Tool: the model removes service with a fixed +0.5 (TCRP average). For a new line's frequency slider use the Table 9-2 headway values by headway band.

## BRT boost, driver share, subway high end (research of Oct 27, 2026)

- BRT corridor riders vs. the bus before: Cleveland HealthLine 16,200 → 21,200 (+31%), run time 46 → 36 min (FTA Before-and-After Studies 2012, pp. 4–5, https://www.transit.dot.gov/sites/fta.dot.gov/files/docs/2012_Before_and_After_Studies_of_New_Starts_Projects.pdf); LA Orange Line 41,580 → 62,597 (+51%) (FTA Report 0004, p. xvi, https://www.transit.dot.gov/sites/fta.dot.gov/files/FTA_Research_Report_0004_FINAL_2.pdf); Las Vegas MAX 7,000 → 9,800 (+40%) (https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/35232/dot_35232_DS1.pdf p. 30); LA Metro Rapid +26–33% (https://www.transit.dot.gov/research-innovation/metro-rapid-demonstration-program-evaluation-report-ridership). FTA CBRT 2009 p. E-6: "5 and 25 percent are common" (https://www.transit.dot.gov/sites/fta.dot.gov/files/CBRT_2009_Update.pdf). Tool: ×1.31, range 1.05–1.51.
- In-vehicle time elasticity: bus −0.4 to −0.6, rail −0.4 to −0.9 (Paulley et al. 2006, https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/2034/1/ITS23_The_demand_for_public_transport_UPLOADABLE.pdf). Not used yet.
- Streetcars, weekday riders per route mile (NTD 2023–24): KC ~2,180, Detroit ~960, Cincinnati ~730, Milwaukee ~640, Portland ~1,060, Tucson ~1,170, Seattle ~1,080. No study shows streetcars beating a same-street bus; tool gives no boost.
- Commuter rail upgrades: Tri-Rail +60% trains, boardings +56% 2005–08 (gas prices too) (FTA 2011 before/after p. 12–13); GO Lakeshore 30-min all-day → +30% off-peak riders (Metrolinx 2014–15 Business Plan p. 25); Caltrain electrification FY25 +47% (pandemic recovery mixed in).
- Subway high end: LA Purple Section 3, $3,599.27M / 2.56 mi = $1.41B/mi (FFGA 2020); Section 2, $2,499.24M / 2.59 mi = $965M/mi (https://transit.dot.gov/sites/fta.dot.gov/files/2024-03/CA-Los-Angeles-Westside-Purple-Line-Extension-Section-3-Profile-AR25.pdf).
- Riders who used to drive (share of all riders): Las Vegas MAX 10% (30% new to transit); LA Orange BRT 25% (16% drove alone + 9% carpool); Expo Phase 2 ~31% (70% new to the line × 44% former drivers); CTA Orange Line ~25% new to transit. Tool: 10–31% of all riders.
- FTA GHG Table A-12 RFCW (Chicago grid): HR 0.00247, CR 0.00466, LR/SR 0.00347 t per car-mile. Diesel CR 0.02717 = NTD gallons × 0.01018 ÷ VMT (2017–21); applied per train in the tool.

## Long-run CO₂ (land-use multiplier)

- Multiplier = total VMT reduction ÷ direct substitution. APTA 2009 default 1.9 (Bailey et al. 2008, ICF for TCRP J-11: https://onlinepubs.trb.org/onlinepubs/tcrp/docs/TCRPJ-11Task3-FR.pdf). Sabouri, Ewing & Kalantari 2024 (Transportation, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11116-024-10542-0): 28 regions, 6.1–9.5, median 6.59, defined as (direct + indirect) / direct; Chicago not in the sample. Earlier estimates (their Table 1): 1.29–9. Tool: 1.9 low, 6.59 high.
