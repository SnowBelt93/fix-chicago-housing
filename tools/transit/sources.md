## Riders who used to drive, trips, emissions

- LA Expo Line Ph 2 survey: 70% new riders, 44% of them used to drive (~31% of all riders). https://www.masstransitmag.com/rail/press-release/12257690/
- CTA Orange Line (TRR 1503, 1995): ~25% of boardings new to transit with a car available. https://trid.trb.org/View/453012
- LA Metro Rapid (BRT-lite): 17% previously made no trip or used a non-transit mode. https://onlinepubs.trb.org/onlinepubs/tcrp/tcrp90v1_cs/LosAngeles.pdf
- Assumption for the tool: 15–30% of riders diverted from driving (low for bus, high for new rail).
- NHTS 2022: 1.5 occupants per household vehicle trip; 11.48 mi average vehicle trip. https://www.energy.gov/cmei/vehicles/articles/fotw-1333-march-11-2024-2022-average-number-occupants-trip-household ; https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/73764/dot_73764_DS1.pdf
- APTA 2023 average transit trip: bus 3.7 mi, heavy rail 4.4, light rail 5.1, streetcar 1.9, commuter rail 22.6. https://www.apta.com/wp-content/uploads/APTA-2025-Public-Transportation-Fact-Book.pdf
- EPA: typical car ~400 g CO2 per mile. https://www.epa.gov/greenvehicles/greenhouse-gas-emissions-typical-passenger-vehicle
- FTA GHG factors (t CO2e per vehicle-mile): heavy rail car 0.00205, light rail/streetcar 0.00289, electric commuter rail 0.00387, diesel commuter rail 0.02717, diesel bus 0.00196, electric bus 0.00104. https://transit.dot.gov/sites/fta.dot.gov/files/2024-04/Greenhouse-Gas-Emissions-from-Transit-Projects-Programmatic-Assessment-Report-0263.pdf
